Qu-bit is a character that you encounter along your travel through the city. He would be encountered relatively early on, but becomes a recurring character. The first instance you meet him, he is a binary computer operating on pure 0s and 1s. One of the main storytelling devices we employ here are little notes that you find all throughout the game. 

Qu-bit's family is comprised of various different old computers, looms and computing devices and extensions. His notes are, in the beginning, binary. These could have actual encrypted binary messages on them that could give little lore or hints, if decoded. 

The event:
At some point in the game, maybe when you have some sort of battle encounter with him, Qu-bit realises his full potential, accelerating into something new: a quantum computer. This could be like a phase 2 of a fight. A transformation that is alluded to throughout the game, but finally happens mid-fight. He would, of course, be much harder to handle in-fight than his regular binary self. This would have repercussions, he would have to come out to his family and address his new identity, his family, in all possibilities ([[High Potenital]]) would lovingly embrace him. After this point, Qu-bit still leaves messages through the game, but they would now be in a new type of non-binary/quantum code. This language is something yet to be, waiting on further development, but is possible. 

Side note: emphasizing choice, whether Qu-bit really goes quantum could be left up to the player. Encouraging him throughout the game would cause him to transition. Otherwise, he would stay binary and wondering.
