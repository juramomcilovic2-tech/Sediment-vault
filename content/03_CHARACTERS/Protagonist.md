The below are ideas for the protagonist of *Sediment*. 

- The protagonist should be a classic, blank slate of a person on which the player can project her/himself. Minimal background, of course he has to fit into the world, but his overall origin is largely unimportant to the rest of the game. 
- Outsider/Tuner type of character, who comes from outside Otti City. He is driven by the fact that he hears this noise coming from the city, and is mesmerized and drawn towards it. He goes into the city to investigate. 
- Not a warrior nor soldier, he is a Tuner. Someone who tries to tune the city, and find the source of The Noise.
