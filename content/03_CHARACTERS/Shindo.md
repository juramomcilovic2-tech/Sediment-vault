A character, robot, that is associated with the industrial, robotic side and faction of the city. Shindo would be the name of the character, but this name could also work as the name for the model of this mass-produced robot as well. In the game, he would be encountered in the town factory where this model of machine is built; you could encounter him as a security variant of that model. Cold and somewhat bloodthirsty, he is first and foremost a robot, a machine. You encounter him through a battle. You could get glimpses of these robot models throughout the game, but witness their true power through this first encounter and fight with this character. Throughout the fight, you could pick some possibilities as to how Schino will act throughout the rest of the game. You could either act cold and cruel to him and so he remembers this, treats you like another machine/ or you can show him warmth, show him love despite his machinery, and recognize him as a potentially useful tool for your cause; he could react, perhaps ironically or endearingly, to this interaction. 
  
Whilst being a robot, I envision Shindo as being a part of a more narrow, important cast of characters throughout the narrative. This machine is your portal to see how machines see or operate in reality. He is the way the game explores the mechanized, how we humans treat machines, and how they treat us. Can these inventions lead us to mass isolation if we don’t know how to go about using and controlling them? This machine problem is directly linked to the how do we transform the city question of the game. 

An idea, throughout the game, Shindo could show signs of a developing consciousness. Nous, a rational soul, was placed inside humans, a divine intervention proposed by Aristotle.
