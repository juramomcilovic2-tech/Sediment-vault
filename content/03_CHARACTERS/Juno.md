Juno is a character who lives in the archive, the city's hub for science, knowledge and progress. Juno is unhappy with her current position in the organization she is a part of, and is desperately looking for ways to change her circumstances
Secretly, Juno has been developing concepts and ideas regarding a mysterious time-bending device. She reminisces over her dead cat, and is trying to use her as a time-travel object.
Juno's story centers around regret, and how to handle it. She's a high achiever but always feels lesser, she believes travelling back in time to make different decisions and erasing past mistakes will fix her current situation.
The game's stance should be that traveling back in time is a highly volatile and dangerous proccess that the player highly discourages when they interact with Juno. Traveling back should not be the answer
Throughout the game, Juno can go 2 ways. One is highly destructive, where she ignores all warnings and feels anxiety closing in on her as she fears absolute catastrophe if she doesn't go through with her plan. Ironically, if this option plays out, this should be one of the biggest catastrophes and one of the saddest moments of the game, Option 2 is where Juno decides to let go of the past and tries to figure out her future from the present, rather than interviening in past affairs. Juno could then appear later on in the game helping out in some fight for example that happens later on in the area like the Mesh.
