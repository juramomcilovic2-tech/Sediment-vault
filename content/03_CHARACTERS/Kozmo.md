An old man-type character, faction-wise, he is definitely a lover and a creative type. Kozmo is an old man who designed city machines long ago, but when you meet him is now long retired. He is tough, has an enormously strong presence, is a peak sensor type of man, and has a long mouth. Tough shell, acts like hot shit, hard to penetrate, disillusioned with the city, thinks people lost their way, that the city made them feel cold, and now people lack connection and find it hard to talk and interact with one another. Chronically sarcastic, Kozmo hides pain behind this rough yet infectious humor; still, he is deeply disturbed by the fact that he helped spawn the machines that he sees made people grow more distant. Yet, he is very soft inside, filled with love, but has no one to give it to; he lives alone.

For most people, he is too much to handle, brilliant, but self-destructive, a man suffering from double standards. Even with all this, Kozmo has remarkable integrity. He is the showy, boastful, impactful type of old man figure who is very loud at gatherings, talks a lot, loves knowing that he is the center of attention, loves what life has to offer, and he exudes enormous charisma; he is most definitely the introvert’s biggest nightmare. Ultimately, this leads to his sorrow. Think of it as a one-way street, for a man with a two-way mind. You collect all this love and attention from your surroundings, but have no idea how to give it back. He is aware of this dilemma, but either doesn’t know how to, doesn’t feel the need to change, or is just plainly stubborn. No single person, for a long time, has stayed with him by his side consistently in order to form a strong bond that matters, that can grow.

This is Kozmo’s story arc; he is a man at a crossroads. You can influence him. He either gives in to his ways, gives in to habit, and feels he is unable and unworthy, and thus stays lonely, driving himself further towards self-destruction, until tragedy strikes. On the other hand, he sees that he is of strong character, and possesses this sort of Agency to take action, and starts learning how to express his love, radiate it in his community, to start feeling more self-worth. His mind becomes clearer. The third, tragic option, is that he becomes aware he can change, even when this is old, but feels it is too late for him. Instead of making big leaps in character, Kozmo chooses to go out in one last hurrah, sacrificing himself for a turning point moment in the story. 

Notes: Kozmo is a hard nut, but you can manage to break that if you’re perseverant. Often acts like everybody is either irritating or stupid, but deep down, you can make him love you, and it will be an incredibly strong but tough bond. When he shows respect, you know it's for real and genuinely earned. 

Kozmo’s character is largely inspired by Frank (Al Pacino) from Scent of a Woman. Through this, I hope to explore the relationships and mechanisms of old men, their grudges, regrets, holding love, and being unable to express it.
