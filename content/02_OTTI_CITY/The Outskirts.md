The Outskirts are the first area that the player encounters and the first in-game location.

The Tuner doesn't find his way to this area more so as he is thrusted into it. After an accident regarding The Cube, the city's public transport system, he crashes near the Outskirts, and starts exploring the desolate area. 
It is a musty landfill zone, a place where [[The Factory]] dumps its waste and failed prototypes, but also where the majority of the city's waste is compiled. While exploring, you find yourself taking in the environment, which is filled with remnants of industrial machinery, thrown out personal belongings, mass trash and dump and all sorts of gadgets and gizmos that no longer have a purpose or are retired. Aside from the litter, you notice abandoned architecture, some of it shows signs of heavy intervention, some of it heavily modified, and the rest in ruins.

In this area, you also have your first encounters with some characters, you learn the main mechanic and gameplay loop here. Through interactions (clues and jokes) you are given hints as to how to get into the nearby facility...
