Game area that is directly linked to [[The Outskirts]]. The Factory is an industrial facility that fuels Otti City in many ways, and produces a wide arrangement of automatons and gadgets. The Outskirts are in practice, the Factory's main landfill and trash destination, a final resting place for failed prototypes, damaged goods, and retired models. 

The area amazes you, and scares you, with how organized it is. Carefully planned out production, neat and orderly, maximum optimization. These days, most of the operations in the facility are highly automated, with sentient beings mostly managing and overseeing the production process. 
The strict nature of it all shakes you up a bit. The area is overrun with sentry units known as the Taurus. You fell quite uneasy around them. 

Factory models and products are directly linked to [[The Archive]], their research and development team produces ideas that physically manifest through production at The Factory.
