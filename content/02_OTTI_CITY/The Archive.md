Otti City's main hub for technology and natural sciences. 
Research departments at the Archive are often in close collaboration with the [[The Factory|Factory's]] management team when developing new projects. You look around and take in the environment. It is sleek, modern, there's weird and sometimes mind-boggling or curvy shapes you can't quite wrap your head around. The atmosphere is definitely more chaotic than The Factory. 

Most of the Archive's recent projects are considered *slop* by many researchers and co-workers. Some reminisce over the past, the older workers tell you of times when they used to work on methods of improving communication and coordination, they mostly work on new butter-churners now.

*"...New what??"* you ask in great confusion. 

New butter-churners, a scientist tells you politely. Researchers have shown how churning butter, as a slow and repetitive process, can extend current attention spans and train the brains of future generations. The scientist sighs after this, it's as he cannot believe what he's currently telling you.
