If [[The Archive]] is a centre for science and tech, the Mesh is Otti City's cultural heart. 
This area is home to educational hubs, festivals, a vibrant night-life, it is often described as a *creative's sanctuary*. 
The Mesh is visually filled with more organic, irregular shapes. Otti City has a strong community of bug-people, that are highly underrepresented in various institutions, many of them left their native cocoons in search of a better life. Other than the creatives, The Mesh is also sanctuary to the bug-people, where they are welcomed and live peacefully with the other residents. Sometimes, fights or smaller uprisings do arise in the area. 

Because of a long tradition of caring for them and welcoming them into the community, the bug-people have an important cultural imprint on the city. You may experience this through architecture and food. 

The Mesh used to be in close collaboration with the Archive, working together on managing the city and solving problems. In recent times, these connections have loosened, the areas operate more autonomously now, the residents being more polarized than ever before.
