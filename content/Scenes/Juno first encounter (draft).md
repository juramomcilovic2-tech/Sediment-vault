This scene takes place in a rusty corner of [[The Archive]], the city's hub for science and progress. It establishes [[Juno]] as a researcher who, despite her high-achieving nature, is consumed by the weight of her own history.

**Location:** *A cramped, lightless maintenance alcove tucked behind a massive Qu-bit server rack. The air is thick with the smell of ozone and the rhythmic clicking of old relays.*

**Visuals:** The space is a mess of unauthorized tech. Unlike the clean, binary logic of the main Archive, this room is "noisy." Scraps of paper are pinned to the walls with frantic calculations. In the center sits a device that looks like [[Dead-Cat teleportation|a prototype]]: wires wrapping around a small, preserved collar with a name tag that glints under a single desk lamp.

(The player enters, pushing aside a heavy curtain of cables. Juno jumps, nearly knocking over a jar filled with unidentified substance)
