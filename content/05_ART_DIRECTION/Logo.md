*Sediment*... is a game.
Games have logos, usually. So here's some ideas.

![[IMG_20251113_0004 1.jpg]]
This was the first sketch for a logo. Blocky type of letters that are very inter-connected and kind of hard to make put, like a sedimented rock. 

![[Snimka zaslona 2026-02-25 115721.png]]
Another more clean version, tall letters that have been slightly altered and adjusted in Illustrator. i like it but feel as though it is too similar to the new Marathon logo, although with less corners.




![[[t×6s]_[1e72373e-afd6-4755-946e-18a742196442]_2026-02-25_12-14-38.png]]

However, I really like this version. It's the logo above but put through a dithering filter. I like it because it keeps the ideas from that clean logo from before and maintains the basic shape, but makes it imperfect. It feels pixelized (or dithered) but not stereotypically, to me it feels, spiky but woven, looks a bit lie a micro-organism or bug, so it also feels digital but biological. I think it captures that feeling pretty well, this is not a clean game, therefore, the logo should reflect this. 

Idea:
The title screen can have this logo somewhere on the screen and can change appearance, either getting more whole or decomposing based on what choices you are making throughout the game. A little, living logo, that reacts to your actions.
