This note presents main design axioms for the game *Sediment.* It is a kind of manifesto for base rules that should impact all, or at least most, decision-making involving the project. They are as follows:

1. **Emotion over elegance:** if an aspect of the game steers more towards elegance than emotion - it should be erased or re-thinked. As such, mechanics should be fun and innovative, but prioritise storytelling as a higher goal.

2. **Connection must be harder:** making friends with a character, and connecting with them, should always be harder (but more rewarding) that choosing straight-forward and hostile routes. Otherwise, the system is wrong. *Sediment* is a game about the struggle to connect.

3. **Metaphors galore:** nothing exists for its own sake. Mechanics should always serve a purpose of teaching the player something, and for it to work in the grander ecosystem of the game's ideas/concepts. Emotional or social meaning.

4. **Characters are not resources:** therefore, the relationships you build with them throughout the game, should not be abused, optimized or grinded. If the player chooses to do so, she/he is met with consequences. You are building trust here. 

5. **Relationships can be hard and messy:** do I have to explain this one. 

6. **Failure has to have meaning:** an option like cutting the chord has to hurt. Not in a sudden Game Over way, in a lingering one. The game goes on, but it carries the scars you left. One could say it is a sort of...

7. **Technology is a logic, not evil:** technology in the world should not be painted as evil. Rather, as tools that can reflect values of the people who created it. It is a blank canvas, cold, efficient, but not evil. Different groups - different priorities. 

8. **The quiet is special:** quiet, intimate moments should be/feel on par with big climactic ones. They should soothe you, stay with you. 

9. **Sound is narrative:** the music and sound design should be able to communicate even without dialogue. The guitar is a stringed instrument, it is a literal metaphor and the voice of the game. Its many variations should paint different tones and moods. 

10. **Deeply personal:** if decisions, choices around the game don't feel in-line with my own experience and feel weird or off; they should be discarded. This game is my love-letter to this chapter of my life that I'm closing when I'm done with my studies. 

11. **More weight, less world:** a smaller world with more meaningful moments and emotional weight is better than a big one that feels hollow. Otti City is about weaving, not aimless wondering. 

12. **The player is a loom:** Sediment is a game where the player is encouraged to make tough, meaningful connections with characters that surround him. It should teach how to make those connections, and stay human in an optimized world.
