The High Potential is a term regarding a key aspect of the world on a meta level. It is the archive, potential for all possible world scenarios, realised or not. It is a collection of all the what-ifs that proceed action and something existing. 

Pure in-game meta logic. On some level, this is also a metaphor for every possible action the player can make. At the same time, a lore device, and a mechanism intrinsic to every game. 

This concept is closely related to [[Agency]], as a tool through which possibilities are act upon.
