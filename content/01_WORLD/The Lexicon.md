Sediment tries to avoid RPG conventions. Instead, it uses terminology and metaphors based on fabric, weaving and sound (also physics).

Instead of Health: maybe **Sanity**

- **Cut the Cord:** Prekinuti toksičnu vezu (agresivno).
    
- **Interfere:** Ubaciti se u tuđi signal/razgovor.
    
- **Sync:** Postići savršen dogovor (pacifist run).
