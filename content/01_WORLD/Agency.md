Agency is the ability of every sentient and conscious being to break patterns. It is the will to act and change one's environments and surroundings. 

This is a logic, a property that lies nowhere on the *good or bad* spectrum. Agency can be used for the most wicked, but also the noblest of intents. This choice is in the hands of the individual. No matter how many hands they have. 

All the possible outcomes that can arise from choices and Agency taken are documented and stored through [[High Potenital]].
