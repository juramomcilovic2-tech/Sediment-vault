Now onto something quite different, but still related. Regarding the game's mechanic of [[Signal Resonance]], I recently stumbled upon this project by LA architect Neil Denari. While I am no architect, Neil Denari is quite interesting to me and I find a lot of his work, especially drafts for various projects, quite inspiring, very bold, flat with limited colours, its pretty great stuff.

Completely unbeknownst to me, I watched a lecture of his and found out about this project for a device he had in the late 80s. The device is literally playing with frequencies and oscilloscopes to make sound visible. I also found out here that he was into bands like Gang of Four, which was really cool tome, I'm always glad when someone likes that band. Needless to say, it's pretty crazy to me how I found out about this guy purely on a visual basis of his work, and ended up finding out he did stuff like this, which is even more relevant to this project! Quite a cool feeling.
