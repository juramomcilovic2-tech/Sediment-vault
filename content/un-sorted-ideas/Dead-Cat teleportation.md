Idea for a mechanic where the player would meet a cat that maybe has an accident somewhere along the line throughout the story. After the accident, and if the player wishes to do this based on actions or decisions he is not happy about, one could use the cat to teleport/go to different previous checkpoints and play things out differently.

This, however, would then need to be very hard to do/pull off or be very elaborate to do, it would have to be well-balanced so the player does not abuse this too much. Just an idea for now.
