Another crucial concept in the world! The Loom is a metaphor for the entire game world and its mechanisms. The Loom is traditionally a machine, a tool for weaving. In this case, the loom is how the characters believe their world operates, in functions on weaving logic, therefore, most terminology is weaving/fabric rooted. 

The Loom is the world, on a meta-level. Its internal mechanisms are ideas like [[High Potenital]] and [[Agency]]. 

More about the loom is explained in the [[Loom Explained]] note.
