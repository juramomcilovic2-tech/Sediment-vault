*"Hey, what's this about?"*

I'm glad you asked, mysterious stranger. 

***Sediment*** is a game in very early development. 
It is a narrative driven, top-down 2D game, where the player experiences a dysfunctional city, filled with characters you grow to like over the course of the game. 

*"...That sounds very vague."*

Indeed it does, then let's take a closer look, shall we?

The player experiences the game through a protagonist called The Tuner *(working title)* who is intrigued by a mysterious Ruckus. Curiosity gets the better of you, you cannot resist the allure of this noise. On your way to get closer to this noise, via public transport, your means of transportation crashes while you took a quick nap in the vehicle. Big mistake. 

You land in The Outskirts, a weird and smelly industrial dump, right outside a fortress that resembles a large factory. You can hear the noise more clearly now, you are getting closer to the source of the Ruckus. Eventually, fuelled by curiosity, and with the help of some newly-made friends, you make your way to:

![[OttiCity.gif|629]]

Otti City is a fast-moving, industrial town. Cold and distant, yet is home to incredible people you meet on your way. It is home to various Automatons, bug-like creatures, and humans. 

You are getting ever so close to that noise, but an obstacle arises. The city grows on you, even though it is a place plagued by problems of differing magnitude. It grows its roots and digs into you. Mostly the people do. 

This is the ultimate narrative of the game, do you stay and help the clusters of characters that are trying to bring about change, or do you go on looking for something else? Building something slowly, or discovering that which is perfect. That's the dilemma. 
Since this is a game, you have a little something called **CHOICE *(agency)***. The game is a sediment of the choices you make, and the player becomes a sediment of their experiences and characters they interact with. In this game, choice should matter, and actions should have lingering consequences. 

*"Neat. How do you play it?"*

In terms of gameplay, Sediment is a ferly traditional top-down game, but a bit different. 
Movement is 4-directional across a 2D map, and your battles are handled as **turn-based encounters.** 

The primary gameplay loop of the game is **Signal Resonance.**


![[SignalResonance1-1.gif|387]]
Instead of souls and such, imagine that every character emits a signal. That's the primary logic of *Sediment.* Think of all the sound and weaving based metaphors used in everyday communication. *Striking a chord with someone, plucking at someone's heart-strings, cutting the cord...* 



Instead of a classical turn-based fight, the game presents the encounters you have with different characters as managing signals. In every encounter, you are presented with **two signals:** one for the player, and one for the NPC. Your three primary options are: 

**TUNE** - the option that is encouraged the most. Tuning is how you align your signal with others, ensuring a stronger, more stable connection.

**INTERFERE** - instead of aligning, you deliberately interfere the other's signal, throwing obstacles their way, like white noise. This renders their signal more incomprehensible and weak. 

**CUT THE CHORD** - the most lethal, straight forward, and cruel option. Optimized for *"winning"*, but greatly frowned upon, with lingering consequences.
